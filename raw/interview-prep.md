## Interview Questions

### Opening
- Tell me about yourself.
- Why are you leaving JPMorgan?
- Why leave without something lined up?
- Your CV shows a gap between Deutsche Bank and JPM. What were you doing?
- You don't have a degree. How do I justify hiring you to my director?
- What do you find difficult?
- You were a software engineer — why finance?

### Competency
- Walk me through a reconciliation you've done and how you investigated a break.
- Tell me about a time you worked under deadline pressure.
- Describe a time you used data or automation to improve a process.
- Why are you leaving JPM / why fund accounting specifically?
- Why do you want to work here specifically?

### Dangerous
- Tell me about a time you worked through a complex NAV break.
- If an investor called asking why their redemption value was lower than expected, what would you do first?
- You've only been in finance for less than two years. Why hire you over someone with fund accounting experience?
- What would you do in your first 30 days?
- How would you handle a month-end close if you hit an unresolved break at 4:30pm?

### Technical
- What is NAV and how is it calculated?
- What happens during a cash reconciliation?
- What are corporate actions and why are they operationally high risk?
- What's the difference between hedge fund and PE fund accounting?
- Walk me through a waterfall calculation.
- What do you know about Geneva?

### Behavioural
- What are your strengths and weaknesses?
- Where do you see yourself in five years?
- Tell me about a time you made a mistake.
- Tell me about a time you had to learn something new quickly.
- How do you handle working with people who operate differently to you?
- How do you stay up to date with financial markets?
- What's your biggest career achievement?
- How do you handle repetitive work?
- Tell me about a time you disagreed with a decision at work.
- Are you applying to other roles?

## Workplace Q-Bank

### ownership
- Who owns the final decision on this?
- If I complete this, what does success look like to you?
- What would you do differently if you were doing this yourself?

### expectations
- What does 'doing this well' look like from your perspective?
- What's the main risk you're trying to avoid here?
- What would make you flag this as a concern vs a success?

### priority
- If I can only do one of these today, which matters most?
- What's the hard deadline on this?
- Is this more important than what I'm currently working on?

### visibility
- Who else should know about this work when it's complete?
- Would it be useful for me to present this in the next team meeting?
- Who are the key stakeholders I should loop in?

### politics
- Is there any sensitivity I should be aware of in how this is presented?
- Are there stakeholders who might push back on this?
- What's the political context I should understand before engaging?

### overwhelm
- I have X, Y, Z competing — can you help me prioritise?
- I want to make sure I'm not missing something critical. Can we do a quick priority check?
- I'm going to give you an end-of-day update — anything I should flag proactively?

### interview
- What does the team structure look like and who would I work most closely with?
- What's the most common mistake junior fund accountants make in their first six months?
- Where is the team in terms of automation and tooling?
- What does progression look like from junior to senior fund accountant here?
- What's the NAV production cadence — daily, weekly, monthly?
- Do you run fund accounting in-house or through a third-party administrator?
- Most Geneva breaks trace back to the Security Master. How do you handle new security onboarding?
